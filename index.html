THE DRAW
How The Draw Works

All 48 teams are divided into four FIFA pots. Each of the 8 players receives exactly 6 teams via an asymmetric draw mechanic.

Half the players receive two top-tier Pot 1 sides and extra Pot 4 wildcards. The other half get extra mid-tier depth from Pots 2 & 3. Neither group has a clear advantage — it’s glamour versus consistency.

The Four Pots
POT 1 3 hosts + 9 highest-ranked nations
POT 2 Ranked 13th–24th
POT 3 Ranked 25th–36th
POT 4 Ranked 37th–42nd + play-off winners
4 players get: 2× Pot 1 · 1× Pot 2 · 1× Pot 3 · 2× Pot 4
4 players get: 1× Pot 1 · 2× Pot 2 · 2× Pot 3 · 1× Pot 4
